Get To Know Us

Empowering organizations through mental health excellence

At Amaze Synergy Ventures, we understand that mental health is the cornerstone of overall well-being. Our mission is to provide compassionate and comprehensive mental health care, empowering individuals to lead fulfilling and balanced lives.

Established in 2011, Amaze Synergy Ventures (ASV) is a leading corporate mental health and well-being consultancy committed to transforming workplaces into psychologically safe, resilient, and high-performing environments. We partner with organizations to integrate evidence-based strategies, strengthen leadership capacity, and cultivate cultures that prioritize mental health, empathy, and growth.

Our mission is to empower businesses to achieve sustainable success through the promotion of emotional intelligence, organizational resilience, and employee well-being — when people thrive, organizations excel.

PRISMA

PRISMA – Psychosocial Risk Management Framework

← Back to All Services

Our PRISMA-aligned program is designed to help organizations proactively identify, assess, and manage psychosocial risks in the workplace, promoting mental well-being, regulatory compliance, and a healthier organizational culture.

This framework provides a structured, data-driven approach through validated assessment tools (LEO26 & EPC23), actionable risk management strategies, and expert guidance from certified Psychosocial Trained Persons (PTPs) in accordance with OSHA 1994 and ISO 45003 standards.

Certification Program

Psychosocial Trained Person (PTP) Certification Program

← Back to All Services

The Psychosocial Trained Person (PTP) Certification Program is an exclusive professional training initiative conducted and certified by Amaze Synergy Ventures (ASV).

This program is designed to equip participants with the essential knowledge and competencies to identify, assess, and manage psychosocial risks within their organizations in alignment with PRISMA standards and DOSH guidelines.

Participants will gain a comprehensive understanding of workplace mental health, relevant legislation, and psychosocial risk management strategies through expert-led sessions and practical case studies. Upon successful completion, participants will be endorsed by ASV as certified Psychosocial Trained Persons, empowering them to champion mental health and well-being within their organizations.

Key Learning Areas

  • Mental health in the workplace
  • Relevant legislation and standards
  • PRISMA scope and objectives
  • PRISMA process and assessment
  • Risk management strategies
  • Practical case studies
  • Test and final assignment

Peer Support

Peer Support Programme

← Back to All Services

The Peer Support Program by Amaze Synergy Ventures (ASV) is a comprehensive, professionally structured training initiative designed to equip individuals with fundamental counseling, communication, and emotional support skills, certifying them as accredited peer supporters within their organizations.

This program aims to build an internal support ecosystem where employees can confidently provide empathetic, effective, and confidential mental health assistance to colleagues facing stress, burnout, or emotional challenges.

Through a blend of theoretical learning, interactive workshops, and role-play simulations, participants will gain the ability to identify early signs of distress, practice active listening, and offer appropriate support or referrals when needed.

The Peer Support Program ultimately fosters a culture of care and psychological safety, reinforcing organizational resilience and promoting a workplace where mental well-being is prioritized at every level.

PFA

Psychological First Aid (PFA)

← Back to All Services

In today's world, Psychological First Aid (PFA) has become an essential skill for both organizations and individuals. This accredited, evidence-based program is designed to reduce immediate emotional distress, promote adaptive coping, and support recovery following traumatic or high-stress events.

Therapy & Counselling

Therapy & Counselling Services

← Back to All Services

Amaze Synergy Ventures (ASV) offers comprehensive therapy and counselling services facilitated by licensed mental health professionals, designed to promote psychological well-being and personal growth across diverse needs.

Our services include individual therapy for personal challenges and emotional regulation, group therapy for shared healing and peer support, and online therapy to ensure accessibility and confidentiality regardless of location.

We also specialize in relationship and premarital counselling, helping couples strengthen communication, manage conflict, and build healthy, resilient partnerships founded on trust and understanding.
